**Study:** How people feel about artworks, and how well a computer model's estimates match those feelings.

**Researchers:** Associate Professor Shih-Yun Lu (principal investigator), Head of the AI New Media Lab, Department of Digital Content and Technology, National Taichung University of Education (NTCU); and Nawazish Hussain, research intern (TEEP programme), AI New Media Lab.

**Contact:** Prof. Shih-Yun Lu, lucia@mail.ntcu.edu.tw, +886-4-22188050; Nawazish Hussain, 9zish.hu7n@gmail.com.

**Who can take part:** adults aged 18 or older.

**What you will do:** look at 22 artworks, say how each one makes you feel, rate a computer model's estimates, and answer a short questionnaire. It takes about 20–25 minutes. There is no payment.

**Your data:** you receive a random participant code. We do not ask for your name, email or any contact details, and we do not record your IP address. Your answers are stored in a private research dataset that only the research team can access, and results are reported only as group totals.

**Your participant code:** after you agree below, you will see your code. Please write it down; it is the only way to find your answers later.

**Voluntary:** taking part is voluntary. You may stop at any time by closing the page, with no consequences.

**Withdrawing your answers:** to have your answers removed, email the researchers with your participant code within 30 days of taking part. Your answers will be deleted and not used. After that date the data are analysed and can no longer be removed. Because we do not collect your name, we cannot remove answers without your code.

**Data retention:** anonymous answers are kept for 5 years after the study is published, then deleted.

**Risks and benefits:** some artworks show sad, frightening or disturbing scenes; you may stop at any time. There is no direct benefit to you, but your answers help us understand how people respond to art.

**Ethics approval:** AI-New Media Art Lab, 10 November 2026. For questions about your rights as a participant, contact the committee at . lucia@mail.ntcu.edu.tw
