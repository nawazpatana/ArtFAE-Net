
**Pilot test: participant information and consent**
Version 1.0, 14 November 2026

**Project:** How people feel about artworks, and how well a computer model's estimates match those feelings.

**Purpose**
You are invited to test the clarity of our instructions, the usability of the application and the study procedures. Your answers are stored by the research team. They will be used as research results **only if** you agree in the optional box below **and** a research ethics committee later approves this use. Otherwise they are used only to improve the study materials and are then deleted.

**Research team**
Nawazish Hussain, research intern (TEEP programme), AI New Media Lab, Department of Digital Content and Technology, National Taichung University of Education (NTCU).
Supervisor: Associate Professor Shih-Yun Lu, Head of the AI New Media Lab.
Contact: Prof. Shih-Yun Lu, lucia@mail.ntcu.edu.tw, +886-4-22188050; Nawazish Hussain, 9zish.hu7n@gmail.com.

**What you will do**
You will view 22 artworks, say how each one makes you feel, rate a computer model's estimates, and complete a short questionnaire. This takes about 20–25 minutes. You will take part on the researcher's computer.

**Who can take part**
Adults aged 18 or older who can read English or Traditional Chinese.

**Voluntary participation**
Taking part is your choice. You may decline or stop at any time without penalty. Your decision will not affect your grades, academic standing, or relationship with NTCU or the research team.

**Possible discomfort**
Some artworks may show sad, frightening or disturbing scenes. You may skip any artwork with the "Skip this artwork" button, or stop by closing the page. There is no expected direct benefit to you.

**Data and privacy**
The application gives you a random participant code. We do not ask for your name or contact details.
We collect: your emotion choices, strength ratings and optional written reasons; your ratings of the model's estimates; your choices between two model versions; your questionnaire answers (age range, optional gender, art experience, country or culture, native language, usability ratings and optional comments); and technical data needed for the study: response times, timestamps, the language you chose, and attention-check results. We do **not** record your IP address or any other device identifier.
Data are stored in a password-protected database on the AI New Media Lab's research computer at NTCU in Taiwan. Only Nawazish Hussain and Prof. Shih-Yun Lu can access them. The university's system administrators can technically access the server but do not view research data.

**How long data are kept**
If you agree to future research use (optional box below) and an ethics committee approves it, your anonymous answers are kept for at least 5 years after publication. In all other cases, your pilot answers are deleted within 30 days of the ethics committee's decision, and no later than 31 March 2027. Server backups that may still hold them are overwritten within a further 31 days. Only anonymous notes about interface problems and completion times are kept to improve the study.

**Stopping and withdrawing your answers**
Closing the page ends your participation. Answers you have already submitted stay saved, marked as incomplete. To have your answers deleted, email Prof. Shih-Yun Lu with your participant code within 30 days of taking part. Because we do not collect your name, we cannot find your answers without your code.

**Payment or costs**
There is no payment, and taking part costs you nothing.

**Ethics status**
This pilot has not been reviewed by a research ethics committee. It tests the study materials before an ethics application is submitted. The main research study will begin only after ethics approval has been obtained, and pilot answers will be used in research only if that approval allows it.
